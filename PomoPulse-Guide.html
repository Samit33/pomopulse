01 — The five buttons

Everything runs from the watch buttons

No touchscreen needed. Three buttons on the left, two on the right — here's what each one does from the main timer screen.

LIGHT

Backlight, as on any Garmin watch. PomoPulse doesn't use it.

UP

Short press — open Focus Stats. Long press — open Settings.

DOWN

Skip the current break and jump to the next work session (Pomodoro only).

START / STOP

Start a session, then pause and resume it. In Pomodoro, starts and pauses breaks.

BACK / LAP

End & save the session. During Pomodoro work it asks before abandoning. From idle, it exits the app.

02 — Choose your rhythm

Two ways to focus

Switch anytime between sessions in Settings. Once a session starts, the mode is locked until it ends.

Freedom · Trust · Flow

Flowtimer Default

“The session ends on your terms, not a timer's.”

  • No set duration. Start, focus, and stop when you're ready.
  • Pause & resume freely — only active time counts.
  • A gentle nudge at 90 minutes, and a 120-minute ceiling.
  • Left paused for 15 minutes? It auto-saves and ends.

Structure · Discipline · Ritual

Pomodoro

“Commit to the block, or abandon it.”

  • A cycle is 4 work sessions — 1 of 4 through 4 of 4.
  • No pausing during work. It runs until the timer ends.
  • A short break after sessions 1–3, a long break after 4.
  • Breaks are optional — press DOWN to skip.
Abandon a Pomodoro block after 10+ minutes and PomoPulse offers to save it as a flow session instead — nothing focused is lost.

03 — The measurement

What the Flow Score means

A single number from 0 to 100, updated every second, built from two signals your Forerunner already reads.

75%

Heart-rate variability

Calm, evenly-spaced heartbeats signal a settled, absorbed mind. Scored against your own baseline, which PomoPulse learns over time.

25%

Stillness

Deep focus is physically quiet. The accelerometer rewards steady stillness and reads fidgeting as a drop in attention.

Three zones

The first minute is a warm-up while sensors settle — it isn't judged. After that, your score falls into one of three zones:

04 — Your first session

Start to summary in five presses

This walkthrough uses the default Flowtimer mode. Wear the watch snugly a finger-width above the wrist bone so it reads your pulse cleanly.

1

Open PomoPulse & press START

The timer begins counting up and the watch starts recording your heart rate and movement. Settle into your work.

2

Watch the arc turn teal

After the one-minute warm-up, your live Flow Score appears. As you sink into focus, the ring shifts from amber toward teal — you're entering the flow zone.

3

Step away? Press START to pause

Paused time is invisible in your stats — only real focus counts. Press START again to resume right where you left off.

4

Finish with BACK

When you're done, press BACK to end and save the session. As long as you focused for 10+ minutes, the summary slides up automatically.

5

Read your summary, then START to dismiss

See how deep you went. Press DOWN to flip to the signal detail, or START to return to the timer, ready for the next block.

06 — Settings & privacy

Tune it to you

Long-press UP from the timer to open Settings.

Timer Mode

Switch between Flowtimer and Pomodoro.

Durations

Set work (15–60m), short & long break lengths — Pomodoro only.

Live Flow

Show or hide the on-screen flow-zone indicator during focus.

Reset Baseline

Forget the learned HRV calibration and start fresh.

Clear History

Erase all saved sessions from the watch (asks first).

Garmin Sync Privacy

Decides whether a finished session is written as a Garmin activity — the one thing that controls where your focus data goes. Your in-app stats are kept either way.

ON — default

The session is saved as an activity and syncs out.

Watch → Garmin Connect → Strava
OFF

Nothing leaves the watch. It stays in PomoPulse's own stats only.

Watch · private
Want sessions in Garmin Connect but not broadcast on Strava? That's a Strava-side choice — set your default activity visibility to “Only You,” since the Garmin→Strava bridge can't filter by type.
